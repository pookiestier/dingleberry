The Dingleberry League

A 10-team dynasty league on Sleeper, running since the 2022 startup draft. It plays full PPR with IDP, so every lineup carries a defensive lineman, a linebacker, a DB and two IDP flex spots on top of the offense. Rosters carry over from year to year. Each summer adds a 4-round rookie draft, and the top 6 teams make the playoffs in Weeks 15–17.
