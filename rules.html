1.0Draft

1.1Inaugural Draft

Inaugural Draft was held in 2022.

1.2Rookie Supplemental Drafts

Rookie Drafts will be held every year on a date and time voted on by the whole league. This vote is held to the League Polls structures. Drafts will be held on the Sleeper app with a 5-minute running clock, which after running out of time, a player will be auto selected and the manager will be put on auto-pick.

Rookie Drafts will be limited to 4 rounds. In the event of the league voting to remove rounds of the draft, the Commissioner is to record who has received a draft pick in the round being removed via trade and allow them to make addition rookie additions in order of their lost picks. These picks must be rookies.

1.3Draft Pick Order

Draft pick order is ordered by reverse standings.

Playoff teams (top 6 teams) will be picks 5-10 based on final playoff standings.

Non-playoff teams (bottom 4 teams) will be picks 1-4 based on final regular season standings.

3.0Roster

3.1Roster Construction

  • This is an IDP fantasy league with the following limits
  • Quarterback (1)
  • Running Back (2)
  • Wide Receiver (2)
  • Tight End (1)
  • Flex W/R/T (2)
  • Kicker (1)
  • Defensive Line (1)
  • Line Backer (1)
  • Defensive Back (1)
  • Flex IDP (2)
  • Bench Slots (10)

3.2Injured Reserve

There are 6 Injured Reserve slots. It allows:

  • Players with COVID-19
  • Players designated OUT
  • Players that are SUSPENDED
  • Players designated NA
  • Players designated DNR/HOLDOUT/OPT-OUT
  • Players designated DOUBTFUL

3.3Taxi Squad

There are 5 Taxi Squad Slots. There is no deadline for the Taxi Squad meaning teams can swap players in and out of the Taxi Squad at any time. Players that have played more than 2 years are no longer eligible for the Taxi Squad.

4.0Player Acquisition

4.1Waivers

Waiver order is determined by reverse standings meaning 10th gets 1st priority, 9th gets 2nd etc. After players are dropped, they are on Waiver for 2 days. Waivers clear each week on Wednesday at 2AM CST.

4.2Free Agents

Dropped players become Free Agents 2 days after being dropped, allowing anyone to pick them up without having to clear the Waiver order.

4.3Trades

The deadline for trades is the 11th week of the season.

The Commissioner has 2 days to review pending trades, and may deny a pending trade if they have reasonable suspicion of collusion. The Commissioner is not required to wait the full 2 days and can approve a trade at any time before then.

Situations involving trades with players that have already played in their game for the week bypasses the review period and get approved the day after the last game of the week. For example, a trade that is accepted by both teams on a Friday involves a player that played their game on the previous day (Thursday) will be approved on Tuesday (assuming the last game of the week is a Monday Night game).

Draft picks may be traded between teams.

If a trade is accepted between two teams but one of the parties wants to cancel the trade before it is reviewed, they can ask the Commissioner to deny the trade but must provide reason. A reason that could be considered an act of collusion will result in the trade being pushed through anyways.

5.0League Management

5.1Adjustments to the Rules and Settings

Rules and Settings must be voted on by the league via a League Poll.

Any wanted changes to the Rules and Settings can be brought up by any of the league members and the Commissioner must put this to vote by the league.

5.2League Polls

League Polls do not require all members to participate.

League Polls will show which members vote for each option.

Changes made via League Polls require 6 votes to pass.

5.3Commissioners

The current Commissioner is Lucas R (@rudedog12)

The League needs to have 6 votes in favor to remove a Commissioner.

To vote in a new Commissioner or any additional Commissioners, they need to be approved with 7 votes in favor.

A Commissioner may appoint an Temporary Commissioner in the case of an emergency, and at the end of the season, either resign or regain the position. A Temporary Commissioner cannot stay in the temporary position beyond the beginning of the next season.

6.0Playoff Structure

Playoffs start in Week 15.

The top 6 teams in the standings make the playoffs.

Seeds 1 and 2 have a first round bye.

Seeding does not reorder at any point in the playoffs.

7.0End of Season Procedure

Commissioner will lock all rosters from the day after Championship Weekend.

Commissioner will start polls that run during roster lock period.

On the day after the Super Bowl, the league will be transitioned into the new year, rosters unlocked, and rule changes put into place.

Draft will be scheduled during NFL Preseason

Offseason ends the Monday of NFL Week 1.

7.1Offseason

The offseason begins with a roster lock that goes into effect the day after Championship weekend, and ends the day after the Superbowl

During offseason (excluding roster lock period), all roster moves are allowed, including adding free agents, waivers, dropping players, and trading.

When upcoming rookies become available, they aren’t allowed to be added to rosters until the draft is completed.

Determining the upcoming season’s schedule is done randomly.

8.0Buy In and Prizes

League buy in is $69 per member every season.

  • 1st place prize is $420
  • 2nd place prize is $200
  • 3rd place prize is $70

Payment will be collected at the end of the season by the commissioner to be divided out to the prize winners.
