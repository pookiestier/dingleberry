# Week 8 Recap: Pukaffrey Survives the Heavyweight Fight

Dingleberries, the showdown at the top delivered. Pukaffrey edged Team kchris9 by 3.17 to go 7-1, a two-game lead on the field. Three of the five games this week came down to one lineup decision, and two of those were decided by fewer than 4 points. Check your benches, people.

## Week 8 Review
- **Week 8's Game of the Week: Pukaffrey 207.20, Team kchris9 204.03.** The No. 1 record beat the No. 1 power team in the week's highest-scoring game. Jordan Love (26.6), Amani Hooker (25.6), and Puka Nacua (25.0) led the way, with Roquan Smith adding 21.25. Pukaffrey even won with one hand tied behind its back: Emmett Johnson scored 29.18 on the bench while Omarion Hampton started and put up 4.7. Team kchris9 got 29.63 from Josh Allen, 27.9 from D'Andre Swift, and 25.8 from Jaxon Smith-Njigba, and still lost. The one that will sting: Kendal Daniels scored 18.75 on the bench while Bobby Okereke started and scored 8.0. That swap alone wins it. Third-highest score of the week, and a loss.
- **Save Me Drake Maye! 205.83, Goff on my Ginkel 180.12.** Bhayshul Tuten (29.9), Drake Maye (25.13), and Jahmyr Gibbs (24.8) powered the week's second-best score, with Patrick Mahomes' 30.3 sitting on the bench as a luxury. Tony Pollard (29.7) and Michael Wilson (26.8) did their part for Goff on my Ginkel, but Jared Goff's 6.87 and Zay Flowers' zero did not.
- **Young Brown Chubb 185.80, Saquon McConkey 182.13.** Joe Burrow went for 44.4, the best individual score of the week. Saquon McConkey lost by 3.67 with Ladd McConkey (13.6) on the bench while Parker Washington started and scored 3.6. Benching your own team name and losing by less than the difference is a special kind of pain.
- **CTE & Felonies 174.65, Cook'n Bijan in Jeanties 156.25.** CTE & Felonies got zeros from Lamar Jackson and kicker Jason Myers and still won by 18.4, thanks to Tee Higgins (37.5) and Brock Bowers (28.2). Bijan Robinson scored 7.6, and the namesake duo of Bijan and Ashton Jeanty combined for 18.9.
- **T-Time 171.22, Tanking is Hard 149.78.** T-Time won despite Jacoby Brissett's -1.33 at QB (he was dropped Tuesday morning). Tanking is Hard left Tyler Allgeier's 40.1 and Kirk Cousins' 32.53 on a 128.98-point bench. Start those two and it's not close the other way. The new name checks out: tanking is, in fact, hard.

## Week 9 Game of the Week: Pukaffrey vs. T-Time
The league's best record against one of its best rosters. Pukaffrey is 7-1, fresh off beating the No. 1 power team, and holds a two-game lead. T-Time is 5-3 and third in the power rankings with 1,543.69 points, the second most in the league.
