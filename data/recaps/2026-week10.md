# Week 10 Recap: Records Fall at Both Ends

Dingleberries, Week 10 broke the scoreboard in both directions. Pukaffrey topped its own season high with 263.52 and became the first team to clinch a playoff spot. Tanking is Hard put up 109.50, the lowest score of the season, and now holds the inside track to the 1.01. In the middle, three teams sit at 5-5, and only points for separates them.

## Week 10 Review
- **Week 10's Game of the Week: Saquon McConkey 225.90, CTE & Felonies 169.87.** The revenge game went the other way again. Ja'Marr Chase (37.1), Parker Washington (29.7), Cam Skattebo (27.2), and Jayden Daniels (24.1) handled it by 56.03, completing the season sweep after the 215.18-166.18 win in Week 5. CTE & Felonies traded for Matthew Stafford last week, then started Aaron Rodgers (14.67) while Stafford put up 23.1 on the bench. Chase Brown's 28.3 sat there too, part of 148.82 bench points. Chuba Hubbard's 34.2 was the lone highlight. Saquon McConkey jumps from 6th to 4th; CTE & Felonies stays 5th, but now only on points.
- **Pukaffrey 263.52, Save Me Drake Maye! 185.08.** A new season high. Christian McCaffrey (53.3, the best score of the week), Brock Purdy (36.07), and Emmett Johnson (34.1) did the damage. Jahmyr Gibbs gave Save Me Drake Maye! 46.2, but Drake Maye scored 1.7 while Patrick Mahomes dropped 45.1 on the bench. Even that swap leaves it 35.04 short.
- **Cook'n Bijan in Jeanties 233.08, Pound the A-Gap 217.88.** George Pickens (43.5) and Dak Prescott (41.13) took down the league's top-scoring team by 15.2. Jaxon Smith-Njigba (34.1) and Amon-Ra St. Brown (28.8) kept Pound the A-Gap close, with Deebo Samuel Sr.'s 29.72 stuck on the bench.
- **Young Brown Chubb 146.13, Tanking is Hard 109.50.** Joe Burrow (40.43) and Isaiah Likely (29.7) were enough despite a 0.0 from Kenneth Walker III. Tanking is Hard's 109.50 is the lowest score of the season. The tank is finally working.
- **Goff on my Ginkel 195.75, T-Time 144.22.** Jared Goff (26.4) bounced back from last week's 1.17, with Tony Pollard (23.9) and Stefon Diggs (20.3) helping out. That's two wins over T-Time this season. Bryce Young scored 3.77, and T-Time's top scorer was a linebacker, Devin Lloyd (19.55).
