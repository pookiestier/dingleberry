# Week 7 Recap: The Perfect Season Dies at the Hands of a Tanker

Dingleberries, the streak is over. Pukaffrey walked into Week 7 at 6-0 and walked out 6-1, courtesy of a team with "Tanking" literally in its name. Meanwhile Team kchris9 dropped another 213 and is quietly running away with the power rankings, and Save Me Drake Maye! finally settled that Week 1 tie.

## Week 7 Review
- **Week 7's Game of the Week: Tanking for J. Smith 192.87, Pukaffrey 172.55.** The league's last unbeaten team went down by 20.32. Alex Singleton (30.85) led Tanking for J. Smith, with Caleb Williams (22.07) and Javonte Williams (21.5) chipping in. The painful part for Pukaffrey: Malik Willis put up 29.63 on the bench while Brock Purdy started and scored 10.1. That swap alone would have closed all but 0.79 of the gap. And Tanking's bench? Another 149.10. This tank has a strange way of winning games.
- **Saquon McConkey 215.75, Cook'n Bijan in Jeanties 177.25.** The top score of the week, led by a linebacker: Blake Cashman with 34.8. Cook'n Bijan left Bo Nix's 31.8 on the bench, but a 38.5-point loss is not a one-swap problem.
- **Team kchris9 213.35, CTE & Felonies 170.42.** A 42.93-point beatdown behind Jaxon Smith-Njigba (31.3) and Amon-Ra St. Brown (25.2). Nico Collins gave CTE & Felonies 2.6, and 118.07 sat on their bench.
- **Goff on my Ginkel 211.95, T-Time 177.03.** Tony Pollard went off for 36.5, the best individual score of the week. One week after the season's lowest score, Goff on my Ginkel put up 211.95, while Bryce Young's 4.33 at QB sank T-Time.
- **Save Me Drake Maye! 174.35, Young Brown Chubb 151.65.** The Week 1 tie finally has a winner. Drake Maye led the way with 29.3, and Joe Burrow's 35.93 for Young Brown Chubb went to waste.
