# Week 7 Recap: The Perfect Season Dies at the Hands of a Tanker

Dingleberries, the streak is over. Pukaffrey walked into Week 7 at 6-0 and walked out 6-1, courtesy of a team with "Tanking" literally in its name. Meanwhile Team kchris9 dropped another 213 and is quietly running away with the power rankings, and Save Me Drake Maye! finally settled that Week 1 tie.

## Game of the Week: Tanking for J. Smith 192.87, Pukaffrey 172.55
The closest game of the week came down to 20.32 points, and it took down the league's last unbeaten team. Alex Singleton (30.85) led Tanking for J. Smith, with Caleb Williams (22.07) and Javonte Williams (21.5) chipping in. The painful part for Pukaffrey: Malik Willis put up 29.63 on the bench while Brock Purdy started and scored 10.1. That swap alone would have closed all but 0.79 of the gap. And Tanking's bench? Another 149.10, including Omar Cooper Jr. (24.5) and KC Concepcion Jr. (23.2). This tank has a strange way of winning games.

## Biggest Blowout: Team kchris9 213.35, CTE & Felonies 170.42
A 42.93-point beatdown. Jaxon Smith-Njigba (31.3) and Amon-Ra St. Brown (25.2) did the damage, with Quinshon Judkins adding 23.4. Tee Higgins (28.8) tried to keep CTE & Felonies in it, but Nico Collins gave them 2.6, and 118.07 points sat on the bench, Caleb Douglas's 24.0 among them.

## Matchup-by-Matchup
- **Saquon McConkey 215.75, Cook'n Bijan in Jeanties 177.25.** The top score of the week, led by a linebacker: Blake Cashman with 34.8. Michael Penix Jr. (25.0) and Bucky Irving (23.0) helped. Cook'n Bijan left Bo Nix's 31.8 on the bench while Matthew Stafford started for 21.2, but a 38.5-point loss is not a one-swap problem.
- **Team kchris9 213.35, CTE & Felonies 170.42.** See above.
- **Goff on my Ginkel 211.95, T-Time 177.03.** Tony Pollard went off for 36.5, the best individual score of the week, and Zay Flowers added 29.7. One week after posting the lowest score of the season, Goff on my Ginkel put up 211.95. T-Time got 27.1 from Trey McBride and 26.1 from Jameson Williams, but Bryce Young's 4.33 at QB was not going to cut it.
- **Tanking for J. Smith 192.87, Pukaffrey 172.55.** Goodbye, perfect season. See above.
- **Save Me Drake Maye! 174.35, Young Brown Chubb 151.65.** The Week 1 tie (176.22 apiece) finally has a winner. Drake Maye himself led the way with 29.3, and Kyren Williams (18.1) and Malik Nabers (19.1) backed him up. Joe Burrow threw up 35.93 for Young Brown Chubb, but Aaron Jones Sr. (2.6), Jaylin Noel (2.67), and Isaiah Likely (3.2) left him stranded.

## Studs & Duds
- **Stud team:** Saquon McConkey, 215.75. Team kchris9 (213.35) and Goff on my Ginkel (211.95) were right behind.
- **Dud team:** Young Brown Chubb, 151.65. The only team under 170 this week, despite the second-best individual score in the league.
- **Best individual:** Tony Pollard, 36.5 for Goff on my Ginkel. Joe Burrow (35.93) and Blake Cashman (34.8) round out the podium.
- **Dishonorable mention:** Saquon McConkey picked up Christian Kirk, started him right away, and got 0.0. They won by 38.5 anyway.

- Saquon McConkey added Christian Kirk (dropping Jaleel McLaughlin). See Dishonorable mention.
