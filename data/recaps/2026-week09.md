# Week 9 Recap: Pukaffrey Drops a 259 Bomb

Dingleberries, Pukaffrey just posted the highest score of the season, 259.48, and beat T-Time by 103.15. The leader is 8-1, two games clear, and apparently still getting better. Elsewhere, Pound the A-Gap (formerly Team kchris9) won ugly, CTE & Felonies and Cook'n Bijan in Jeanties made a trade, and the 6th seed is up for grabs.

## Week 9 Review
- **Week 9's Game of the Week: Pukaffrey 259.48, T-Time 156.33.** So much for a heavyweight fight. Puka Nacua (36.5), CeeDee Lamb (36.5), Christian Watson (31.1), and Brock Purdy (26.73) turned this into the season's biggest score, and Pukaffrey did it with Jordan Love's 39.7 sitting on the bench. T-Time got 25.75 from Nakobe Dean, but Josh Jacobs went for -0.5 and Tucker Kraft for 1.9, and Bryce Young's 21.5 sat on the bench behind Tyler Shough's 13.93. T-Time left 136.20 on the bench. It would not have mattered.
- **Young Brown Chubb 223.73, CTE & Felonies 201.40.** Kenneth Walker III (36.3) and Joe Burrow (34.73) did the heavy lifting, with Luther Burden III adding 25.2. Brock Bowers (22.3) and Tee Higgins (20.2) kept CTE & Felonies close, and newly acquired Matthew Stafford chipped in 10.7.
- **Saquon McConkey 219.53, Tanking is Hard 172.60.** Ja'Marr Chase (33.5), Arvell Reese (29.5), and Bucky Irving (27.5) cruised to a 46.93-point win. Last week Ladd McConkey scored 13.6 on the bench; this week he started and scored 0.0. Carson Schwesinger's 29.85 was the bright spot for Tanking is Hard.
- **Cook'n Bijan in Jeanties 204.72, Save Me Drake Maye! 146.92.** Matthew Golden (34.4) and Bijan Robinson (25.7) snapped Save Me Drake Maye!'s three-game win streak by 57.8, the second-biggest margin of the week. Drake Maye's 18.27 led the losing side.
- **Pound the A-Gap 140.74, Goff on my Ginkel 126.57.** The lowest-scoring game of the week. Pound the A-Gap started Josh Allen (9.3) while C.J. Stroud put up 47.8 on the bench, the best individual score of the week by anyone. Its season-low score still won, because Jared Goff scored 1.17.

## Trade Alert
