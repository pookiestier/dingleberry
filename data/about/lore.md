## 2022 · The Founding: Thirty Rounds of Bad Decisions

On **July 31, 2022**, ten managers sat down for a **29-round** startup snake draft, all 290 picks of it. With the **No. 1 overall pick, Cris Collinsworth took Jonathan Taylor.** Team selahhelah followed with Ja'Marr Chase and Team VanillaRice12 with Justin Jefferson. I took Cooper Kupp at 1.05, and Jimbonertesticlenutblast quietly grabbed Christian McCaffrey at 1.08. He has never been traded since.

Jonathan Taylor didn't last long. On **October 8, 2022**, Cris Collinsworth sent the 1.01 to Joe Shiesty for Alvin Kamara, Allen Lazard and Joe Shiesty's 2023 first. Taylor is still on that franchise today. He dropped 55.10 points for it in Week 10 of 2025, when it was called Waddle Service. Patience is a virtue, apparently.

IDP scoring was very generous in year one. On top of the base point per tackle, a solo tackle was worth a full extra point, an assist another full point, and a tackle for loss two. The scoreboard went wild. **Pierce'd Tiddies (that's me)** put up **3,200.77 points**, still the most in a single season. In Week 5 I hung **290.03** on Two Woke Cucks, which is both the highest score and the biggest blowout (133.83) in league history. Frame it. I did.

Team boettcher19 won the regular season at **10-4**, with Joe Shiesty also 10-4. In the playoffs, Pierce'd Tiddies went in as the 3 seed and beat Two Woke Cucks 231.85–158.65. Next came Joe Shiesty, 257.42–238.90. The final was against Team boettcher19, **216.33–119.83**. **The first Dingleberry champion was your commissioner.** I'd like to say this was the start of a dynasty. Please keep reading anyway. Team selahhelah took third place.

At the bottom, Micah Parsons Project went **2-12**. It then lost the Toilet Bowl final to Cris Collinsworth by 0.96 points. Keep an eye on this franchise.

## 2023 · The Jimbonertesticlenutblast Era

The league fixed the defense. Solo tackles dropped to 0.5, assisted tackles to 0.25 and tackles for loss to 0.5, with 0.2 added per QB hit. So any 2022 score deserves an asterisk.

New blood arrived too. **ParkerEvjen took over Purger14's franchise** and kept the Cris Collinsworth name for one more year.

The offseason was a frenzy, with 15 trades before March 18. DJ Moore alone was traded **four times in 10 days**. He went from me to Burrow it in your mom on February 2, then to Team VanillaRice12 on February 8, then back to me on February 10. On February 12 I traded him to Jimbonertesticlenutblast, where he scored 55.00 in Week 5. Love that for me.

The rookie draft ran five rounds that year; it has been four ever since. Micah Parsons Project, rewarded for being bad, took **Bijan Robinson at 1.01**. I took Jahmyr Gibbs at 1.02, and Team kchris9 took Jaxon Smith-Njigba third.

Then **Jimbonertesticlenutblast** went **12-2** and scored **3,058.26**. That's the best total of the reduced-defense era. They beat Burrow it in your mom 214.78–140.22 in the semis and Team kchris9 **249.97–186.53** in the final. Team selahhelah took third again, its second straight podium finish, and then handed the franchise to Piorkowski11.

My own season, as A Pee Kupp, went 5-9. The highlight was beating Cris Collinsworth **164.67–164.58**, the closest decided game ever. That's a nine-hundredths-of-a-point win, and yes, I'm counting it.

## 2024 · Legette This Bread

**Piorkowski11** showed up and went 11-3 right away. So did **ParkerEvjen's franchise, now called Legette This Bread**, which won the regular season on points (2,888.81).

The trade machine kept humming. Wyattdeterman's franchise has a naming tradition: name the team after a guy, then trade the guy. It started out as Joe Shiesty and drafted Joe Burrow. On January 31, 2023, it traded Burrow to a team literally called Burrow it in your mom. In February 2023 it landed Joe Mixon and Jaylen Waddle and played that season as **Mixon it up**. On March 25, 2024, it shipped Mixon, Drake London, Gabe Davis and three 2025 second-rounders to Legette This Bread for **Breece Hall** and became **BreeceMode**. It made 12 trades in the 2024 league year, more than anyone. It has made 34 all-time, also a league high. Team kchris9 has made two.

Micah Parsons Project took **Marvin Harrison Jr. at 1.01**, its second straight No. 1 pick. That One Shitty Team took Caleb Williams at 1.02, and in the fourth round it also took a quarterback named Drake Maye at No. 32 overall. Remember that one.

The playoffs were tight. Legette This Bread survived Team kchris9 **181.05–179.88** in the semis. In the final it beat Team Piorkowski11 **221.95–217.77**. **The franchise that drafted Jonathan Taylor first overall finally had its title**, two years and one manager later. Team kchris9 took third.

Two more for the archive. In Week 10, Ja'Marr Chase scored **63.40** for Team Piorkowski11, tied for the best player game ever. They still lost, 210.27–196.43, to my team, then called Awtizm. And Micah Parsons Project hit the bottom of the Toilet Bowl again, losing its final to me by 2.06 points.

## 2025 · Fool's Gold Turns to Gold (Almost)

Micah Parsons Project became **Fool's Gold** and used its **third straight No. 1 pick** on Ashton Jeanty. Then the 7-7 squad snuck in as the **6 seed** and got hot. It beat CTE & Felonies 231.37–199.40 and Team kchris9 238.05–177.06 to reach the final. From the 1.01 to the championship game.

There it ran into **Pukaffrey**, the renamed Jimbonertesticlenutblast franchise. Pukaffrey went **11-3**, dropped **281.73** in the semifinal (the second-highest score ever) and won the final **191.50–158.82**. **That's two titles, making it the league's first two-time champion franchise.** Waddle Service had the most points (2,796.07) and took third.

My She Bledsoe I'm Good team went 6-8. In Week 12, Jahmyr Gibbs, my 2023 pick, scored **63.40** to tie Chase's record in a 211.03–105.10 win. In Week 16, also me, I scored **97.49**, the lowest score in any real league game (regular season or bracket). That came in the Toilet Bowl final, which I lost. From the penthouse to the porta-potty in four seasons.

## 2026 · The Story So Far

The 2026 rookie draft opened on August 15. **Goff on my Ginkel**, formerly Burrow it in your mom and coming off a 3-11 season, took **Jeremiyah Love at 1.01**. I took Makai Lemon at 1.04.

On **September 18**, I sent Makai Lemon, Kyle Monangai, and my 2027 *and* 2028 firsts to Tanking for J. Smith. In return I got **Drake Maye**, Kyren Williams and a 2028 second, and the team name finally makes sense. In Week 1, Save Me Drake Maye! and Young Brown Chubb tied **176.22–176.22**, the **first tie in league history**.

Through six weeks, **Pukaffrey is 6-0**, the only unbeaten team. Team kchris9 (4-2) leads the league with 1,283.58 points. CTE & Felonies and T-Time are also 4-2. On September 7, Saquon McConkey (Piorkowski11's franchise, under its first custom name) sent Derrick Henry, Jauan Jennings and a 2027 first to CTE & Felonies for Rashee Rice and Cam Skattebo. Down in the basement, Goff on my Ginkel is 1-5, and Save Me Drake Maye! is 1-4-1 despite ranking sixth in points. I'm told that's called "variance." Week 7 is under way.
